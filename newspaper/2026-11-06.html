MAJOR NEWS UPDATES

WORLD

Canada proposes extending exploration tax credits to 2032

Canada's 5 November budget proposes extending the 15% Mineral Exploration Tax Credit and 30% Critical Mineral Exploration Tax Credit to March 2032, with antimony added to the enhanced incentive. These are budget proposals, not disbursed project finance. Longer eligibility could improve junior exploration financing conditions.

INDIA

India launches 20 critical-mineral blocks and 11 exploration-licence blocks

The Ministry of Mines confirmed on 5 November the ninth tranche of 20 critical/strategic mineral blocks in eight states, including rare earths, graphite and vanadium, and the third tranche of 11 exploration-licence blocks in six states. ANVESH and EKAM data-governance portals were also launched. Auction launch is not a licence award; review bid documents for exact eligibility and deadlines.

5 November 2026 · Original source / reporting

Goa mining ministers' conference advances coordination agenda

The 5–6 November conference began with federal-state discussion on exploration, mineral processing, recycling and technology. The official 5 November report documents initiatives but not an executed downstream procurement contract. Monitor day-two decisions rather than treating policy intentions as achieved production.

5 November 2026 · Original source / reporting

TANZANIA

Total Graphite proposes acquisition of operating Tanzanian graphite processor

Total Graphite's 5 November RNS reports signed heads of terms for a Tanzanian reprocessor with a stated 12,000 tpa operating plant and another 12,000 tpa plant under construction. Upfront share consideration is £1.88m, with up to approximately £4.7m deferred contingent consideration; a separate £0.41m convertible loan-note raise was announced. The acquisition remains conditional on due diligence, documents and approvals. This is neither Sakariya's Tanga project nor proof of future production capacity.

5 November 2026 · Original source / reporting

Tanzania mining investment conference approaches: continuing watch

The previously announced Tanzania Mining and Investment Conference is scheduled for 19–21 November in Dar es Salaam, with sessions on graphite, value addition and project financing. This is a continuing calendar catalyst, not a newly announced event within the last 24 hours; confirm registration and meeting availability directly with organizers.
