Brazil challenges EU scrutiny of nickel asset transaction

Reuters reporting dated 18 November describes Brazil's objection to European Commission review of Anglo American's previously announced US$500 million Brazilian nickel asset sale to MMG. The underlying sale was announced in 2025; the new development is regulatory friction, not a new deal. Cross-border control of ferronickel supply remains a transaction risk.
