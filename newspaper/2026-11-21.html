TANZANIA / REGIONAL

Tanzania signals tougher enforcement on idle mineral licences

Reporting on 20 November from the mining conference says the vice-president warned holders of undeveloped mineral licences of enforcement and reiterated domestic value-addition policy. Officials identified delays affecting graphite, nickel, helium and niobium projects. This is a policy signal, not evidence that any specific Sakariya licence has been challenged.

Source / original report · Publication 18–20 November 2026 · New conference reporting

Tanzania approves mineral survey fund, targets high-resolution coverage

At the 19 November conference, officials described approval to allocate 10% of mineral revenue to geological surveying. High-resolution airborne geophysics reportedly covers 16% of Tanzania, with a 50% target by 2030. The funding mechanism is announced; procurement packages or tender eligibility were not independently verified.

Source / original report · Publication 18–20 November 2026 · New conference reporting

Nyanzaga gold commissioning advances; first gold still prospective

A 20 November project update reports first ore fed to the Nyanzaga plant, commissioning under way and first gold targeted for early 2027. Its February 2026 reserve estimate is 90.9 Mt at 1.38 g/t for approximately 4.0 Moz. Ore introduction is not commercial production; the report reprises an earlier commissioning milestone.
