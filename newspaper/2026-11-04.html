WORLD — World Gold Council reports stronger official-sector buying, weaker retail demand

3 Nov 2026 · Source / verification ↗

The World Gold Council’s Q3 2026 report records central-bank net purchases of 321 tonnes (+42% year-on-year) and gold ETF inflows of 211 tonnes. Bar-and-coin demand fell 19% to 268 tonnes and jewellery consumption declined 17% to 313 tonnes. Mine production rose 3% to a third-quarter record 1,071 tonnes; these are Q3 observations newly published on 3 November, not 24-hour production changes.

Why it matters: Gold exploration investor presentations should distinguish resilient institutional demand from weaker physical consumer demand. A higher gold price does not automatically translate into stronger mine financing.

INDIA — World Gold Council sees Q3 demand down 29% year-on-year

3 Nov 2026 · Source / verification ↗

The Council’s India Q3 report estimates total demand at roughly 162 tonnes, 29% below Q3 2025, despite a quarter-on-quarter recovery. Jewellery consumption was about 100 tonnes (-20% year-on-year), while bar-and-coin investment was 55 tonnes (-40%). The report gives the quarterly average domestic gold price as INR148,891 per 10g, up 45% year-on-year; it is not today’s spot retail price.

Why it matters: India remains an important demand and investment reference for gold-market narratives, but volume and rupee value should not be conflated.

INDIA — Bhalukona nickel-copper-PGE modelling: useful technical comparator, not a declared resource

1 Nov 2026 disclosure; 2–3 Nov media follow-up · Source / verification ↗

Deccan Gold’s 1 November stock-exchange-linked release described preliminary potential for more than four million tonnes of mineralisation around 0.24% nickel-equivalent, informed by 15 drillholes totalling 2,773 metres. The estimate covers only part of a 1.3-km corridor; magnetic and induced-polarisation work indicates possible extensions. It is explicitly preliminary geological modelling, not a compliant mineral resource or reserve, and the company announcement predates this 24-hour window.

Why it matters: Relevant to exploration workflow and public disclosure discipline: show drilling, geophysics and resource confidence separately.
