MAJOR NEWS UPDATES

WORLD

Guinea grants Bankan exploitation permit

Guinea granted PDI Gold the final exploitation permit for its Bankan gold project after an extended wait. The reported US$463 million development is described as financed; management anticipates construction in four to six weeks, not production today. Permitting resolution is an important financing and schedule catalyst for West African juniors.

Reported 17 November 2026 · Source and verification

US critical-metals startup announces $128 million financing

General Flash reportedly raised US$128 million in seed and Series A equity led by Lux Capital, Construct Capital and Overmatch Ventures for a metal-oxide reduction technology targeting metals including tungsten and titanium. Financing is reported; commercial production and cost competitiveness are not established by this announcement.

Reported 17 November 2026 · Source and verification

Rio Tinto agrees $250 million Panguna remediation contribution

Rio Tinto agreed to contribute US$250 million toward beginning remediation of legacy impacts at the closed Panguna mine in Bougainville. The amount is a remediation commitment, not a mine restart or exploration investment. The case underscores enduring ESG and community liabilities.

Reported 17 November 2026 · Source and verification

INDIA

Rajasthan mine closures highlight environmental-clearance bottleneck

A 17 November report says approximately 12,000 leased mines in Rajasthan remain shut amid environmental-clearance delays. This is a reported ongoing disruption, not evidence that all closures began yesterday. Clearance timelines remain a material project-execution risk.

Reported 17 November 2026 · Source and verification

India joins Pax Silica supply-chain initiative

A 17 November report says India joined the US-led Pax Silica initiative aimed at strengthening strategic technology supply chains. Specific mineral procurement contracts or mine funding were not established in the report; monitor follow-on implementation rather than treating a declaration as an offtake.

TANZANIA

Mining investment conference confirms 1,715 participants

On 17 November, Tanzania's minerals minister reported 1,715 confirmed participants, including 226 international delegates, for the 19–21 November Tanzania Mining and Investment Conference in Dar es Salaam. This is a newly reported participation figure, not a claim of completed investment deals. Graphite value addition and project financing are relevant meeting themes.
