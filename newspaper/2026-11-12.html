INDIA

India prohibits lithium-ion battery black-mass exports

Reuters reported 11 November that India changed the export classification of lithium-ion battery black mass from free to prohibited, with immediate effect. The secondary feedstock contains recoverable lithium, nickel, cobalt and manganese. The change could increase feedstock retention for Indian recyclers; actual plant utilization and pricing effects remain unverified.
