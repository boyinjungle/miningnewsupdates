MAJOR NEWS UPDATES

WORLD

Hindustan Zinc outlines ₹800 billion expansion

Reuters reported on 16 November that Hindustan Zinc plans roughly ₹800 billion of investment over three to five years, targeting metal production capacity of 2 million tonnes annually versus 1.1 million today. This is a planned investment and capacity target, not funded expenditure or achieved output. Steel and infrastructure demand are the stated drivers.

Canadian Copper reports C$9.78m warrant proceeds

Canadian Copper reported completed warrant exercises yielding C$9.78 million and treasury above C$24 million for its New Brunswick assets. A combined Murray Brook–Caribou feasibility study is targeted for late January 2027; that study is prospective, not completed. Junior explorers should distinguish cash proceeds from projected study milestones.

16 November 2026 · Source / further verification

Cosmos nickel asset transaction proposed with equity consideration

Reporting dated 16 November describes IGO's binding agreement to sell Cosmos to Gorilla Gold in exchange for a 19.9% equity stake and performance rights, subject to transaction terms. This is an asset divestment agreement, not evidence that closing has occurred. Rehabilitation liabilities and retained lithium rights are material to assessing transaction value.

16 November 2026 · Source / further verification

Zambian copper expansion draws scrutiny of local benefits

A 16 November Al Jazeera report examines investment in Zambia's copper sector alongside workers' rights, environmental concerns and local business participation. This is reported sector context, not a newly opened tender. It reinforces the importance of community benefit and local procurement in African mining projects.

INDIA

Mahanadi Coalfields IPO priced for 19–23 November

Reuters reported a ₹129–136 per share price band for the Mahanadi Coalfields IPO, scheduled for 19–23 November. Coal India is selling existing shares; the offering is not new capital raised by the subsidiary. The transaction is a notable example of mining-asset capital-market monetisation.

16 November 2026 · Source / further verification

India's coking-coal import exposure remains acute

A 16 November industry analysis reported FY2025–26 coking-coal imports of 66.33 Mt and approximately 91.79% import dependence, citing parliamentary data. Domestic coal quality and beneficiation yields remain obstacles; this is a steel raw-material security story, not a new graphite procurement order.
