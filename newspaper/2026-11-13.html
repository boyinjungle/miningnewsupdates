Atalaya completes Tharsis acquisition in Spain

Atalaya acquired Tharsis Mining for €125 million, comprising €75 million upfront and €50 million deferred. The past-producing Tharsis and La Zarza properties contain reported polymetallic resources; these are not operating production. This is an executed acquisition, not an open asset sale.

INDIA

India's battery black-mass export prohibition draws fresh coverage

DGFT Notification 49/2026-27 dated 10 November changed export status for specified battery waste under HS 85491400 from Free to Prohibited. Coverage on 12 November is new, but the underlying policy decision predates this 24-hour issue. It may increase domestic recycler feedstock availability; it does not establish any new graphite offtake.

TANZANIA

Imwelo gold project starts 6,500-metre grade-control programme

Lake Victoria Gold's Imwelo project began grade-control drilling on 4 November, reported 12 November. The programme targets the upper 20 metres of Area C on a 10 m by 10 m staggered grid, with completion anticipated February 2027. Grade-control drilling supports mine planning; it is not new gold production. The detailed drilling grid offers a relevant operational comparator for Jomu, not evidence of Jomu's status.
