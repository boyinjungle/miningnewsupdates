INDIA

4 Nov 17:38 IST · Government broadcaster / PIB · INDIA

India approves ₹2,814 crore critical-mineral processing support

The Union Cabinet approved a five-year capex support programme for four proposed plants: two lithium units of at least 30 kt each and two nickel units of at least 50 kt each. Support is 30% of eligible investment, capped at ₹500 crore per lithium beneficiary and ₹900 crore per nickel beneficiary, selected through technical qualification and financial bidding. This is scheme approval, not four funded or operational plants. The two-stage selection is a future opportunity to monitor when official bidding documents appear.
