United States auctions first deep-sea mineral exploration leases

Reported 19 November: Wetstone U.S. Ventures won two provisional seabed lease bids near American Samoa at US$3 million each. The leases permit preliminary investigation, not commercial mining, which requires further approvals. Legal and environmental challenges continue. This is an early-stage strategic supply-chain signal, not new mineral production.

TANZANIA / REGIONAL

Kenya names preferred bidder for Mrima Hill rare earths and niobium

Reported 19 November: Kenya selected the Frontier Minerals consortium as preferred bidder for Mrima Hill in Kwale County. This is not a mining licence: community consultation, consent and prospecting approvals remain outstanding. Competing bidders have challenged the process; the sacred forest and prior environmental disputes make implementation uncertain. For East African explorers, the distinction between preferred-bidder status and permitted project is material.
